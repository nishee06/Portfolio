Who I Am

I'm an MCA graduate from ITM University with a deep love for systems software. I thrive in the terminal — writing C programs that talk directly to sockets, crafting Bash scripts that automate the mundane, and debugging gnarly issues with journalctl and dmesg.

My experience spans from low-level TCP/IP socket programming in C to full-stack web applications in Django, multi-threaded Python utilities, and containerization with Docker. I'm equally comfortable at the kernel boundary and in a modern web framework.

I hold a Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) certification and have completed multiple Linux Foundation and Cisco Networking Academy courses, reinforcing my belief that understanding the foundation is what separates good engineers from great ones.
